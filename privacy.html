Privacy Policy

Placeholder content. The final policy is supplied by Cattermoles Butchery before launch.

1. What we collect

When you place an order or contact the shop we may collect your name, phone number and order details so we can prepare your order and let you know when it is ready.

2. How we use it

Your details are used only to fulfil orders, run home kill bookings and respond to enquiries. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

3. Website

This website does not use contact forms and does not store personal information. Standard server logs and an embedded Google Map are the only third-party services in use; Google's own privacy policy applies to the map.

4. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Ask in store or ring 03 327 8219.

5. Contact

Privacy questions can be raised with the team at 159 Williams Street, Kaiapoi, or by phone on 03 327 8219.
