Judged the best sausage in the country

Fred Hicklin put a jalapeno, smoked cheese and ham sausage into the 2024 Dunninghams Great New Zealand Sausage Competition. It came home gold, and supreme champion of its class. It was only the second year the shop had entered.

The recipe is still made here on Williams Street, in the same room as everything else in the cabinet.
