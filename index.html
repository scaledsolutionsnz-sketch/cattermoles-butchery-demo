Williams St, Kaiapoi

A name Kaiapoi knows

Cattermoles is the butcher shop on Williams Street where the meat is cut on site and the person serving you knows exactly where it came from. Owner Fred Hicklin and the team make everything in-house, from the daily cabinet through to the smallgoods.

The sausages speak for themselves. At the 2024 Great NZ Sausage Competition, up against more than 800 entries, the shop's jalapeno, smoked cheese and ham sausage took the gold medal in its class, backing up a silver the year before.
